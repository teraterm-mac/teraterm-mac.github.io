Uninstallation

Uninstalling from the menu

Choose Tera Term Mac menu > "Uninstall Tera Term Mac…".
A dialog asks what to remove (the dialog is currently shown in Japanese). Check the items and click the first button ("アンインストール", Uninstall).

  • The app (moved to the Trash)
  • Settings and the connection list (including launch at login)
  • Saved passwords (Keychain)

All connected windows are closed, and Tera Term Mac quits after removing anything.
This menu item is shown only when the app is installed in the Applications folder (/Applications or ~/Applications). It is hidden when the app runs directly from the disk image or the Downloads folder.

To keep your connection list, save it first from the TeraTerm Menu list settings with "ttpmenu.ini" > "Export…".

Manual uninstallation

Quit Tera Term Mac and delete the following:

  • The app: /Applications/Tera Term Mac.app (move it to the Trash in Finder)
  • Settings and the connection list: ~/Library/Preferences/net.satoru.teramac.plist
  • Launch at login: ~/Library/LaunchAgents/net.satoru.teramac.menu.plist
  • Saved passwords: the "Tera Term Mac メニュー" items in Keychain Access
  • Setup folder: ~/Library/Application Support/Tera Term Mac (contains TERATERM.INI and other files saved with "Save setup")

The setup folder is not removed by the menu item either. Delete it by hand if you no longer need it.
